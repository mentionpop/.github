# MentionPop

**See where AI picks your brand. Fix where it doesn't.**

MentionPop is an Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) analytics platform. It helps founders, marketers, and agencies measure how brands appear across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI answer surfaces.

## Why we built it

After launching two SaaS products, founder **Aditya Kumar** struggled to understand why useful products remained invisible across search and AI answer engines. Traditional SEO tools showed rankings, but they could not explain why AI recommended competitors or which sources shaped those answers. MentionPop was built to provide that missing evidence.

## What MentionPop provides

- AI mention and recommendation tracking
- Exact citation URL and source extraction
- Competitor share-of-voice evidence
- AI crawler, `robots.txt`, `llms.txt`, and schema diagnostics
- Site-aware auto-fix packs for JSON-LD, FAQs, and comparison pages
- Digital PR and earnable-source opportunity mapping
- White-label, client-ready reports
